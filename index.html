The reward loop

Paid for work, settled every epoch

An epoch closes every 6 hours. Each close settles the epoch's jobs, pays nodes in $IDLE and pays holders in tokenized NVDA from the Holder Vault. Planned for launch, numbers are a draft.

Epoch · preview--:-- UTC

Next close in —

  1. Nodes01

    Work rounds

    Every verified job draws a random 100–1,000 $IDLE from the node reward pool, which the team fills with $IDLE bought on the market. Rewards build up in your node balance, held in the NodeRewards contract, and become instantly available when the IdleNode team triggers the network close onchain.

    100–1,000 $IDLE per job

  2. Builders02

    Pay in $IDLE

    Builders can pay for jobs in $IDLE at 10% under the USDG rate. That $IDLE goes into the Holder Vault with every other job payment.

    10% under the USDG rate

  3. Holders03

    Holder Vault

    The vault fills from every job payment, slashed stakes and team buys. Each epoch it pays tokenized NVDA to every wallet holding at least 100 USD of $IDLE, split by balance and time held. Sent automatically.

    Paid in tokenized NVDA

  4. Everyone04

    Proof ledger

    Every verified job leaves a work receipt. Each epoch's receipts roll up into one root posted on Robinhood Chain, so anyone can check a payout against it.

    One root per epoch, onchain
